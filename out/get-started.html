Get started

Set up Synced in under 5 minutes.

Sign in, connect Gmail, and start reviewing what Synced already handled for you. Pick your platform below.

Connect a Signal
Synced drafts an Action Plan
You clear Decide

Web

3 minutes, desktop only

  1. 1
    Open mail.syncedinbox.com

    Synced on the web is a desktop app. Use a full-size screen.

  2. 2
    Verify your number

    Enter your phone number. Synced texts you a 6-digit code.

  3. 3
    Add your name

    First time only, so your AI agent knows what to call you.

  4. 4
    Connect Gmail

    Add more Signals like Outlook, Slack, or Teams anytime from Settings.

  5. 5
    Check Messages, Decide, and Tasks

    Messages is your inbox. Decide holds approvals. Tasks tracks what's delegated to you.

On a phone browser, you'll be pointed to the mobile app instead.

Mobile

3 minutes, iOS

  1. 1
    Install Synced

    iOS today, with Android on the way.

  2. 2
    Verify your number

    Same sign-in as web. Enter your number, then the code Synced texts you.

  3. 3
    Add your name

    First time only, so your AI agent knows what to call you.

  4. 4
    Connect Gmail

    Required to finish setup. Add Outlook, Slack, or Teams whenever you're ready.

  5. 5
    Open Home

    Home is your conversation list. Tasks holds what's delegated to you. Answer is Decide, built for on the go.

Same phone number as web. Your Signals and history carry over, no separate pairing step.

Good to know

A few terms you'll see.

Signals

The inboxes and channels you connect: Gmail, Outlook, Slack, Teams, and more.

Action Plan

What Synced generates from a message: the steps it takes, or proposes taking, to get it done.

Decide

Your queue of pending approvals and questions. Anything Synced needs your judgment on.

Tasks

Human to-dos. Work a conversation delegates to you directly, tracked in one list.

One account, every device

Sign in with the same phone number on web and mobile and your Signals and history follow you.

Phone verification

Synced uses your phone number, not a password, to sign you in on either platform.
