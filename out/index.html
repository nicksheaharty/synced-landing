How Synced works

Email as easy as
iMessage.

Synced learns what you need, finds the information, drafts responses, and manages follow-ups, all while asking for input only when it matters.

Find information instantly

Synced searches your email, files, and cloud storage without you leaving the conversation, and pulls in whatever context it needs.

Draft replies and documents

Synced drafts email replies and creates documents for you, ready to review and send the moment you approve.

Coordinate schedules effortlessly

Synced checks calendars, finds times that work, and proposes meetings, so you skip the back-and-forth.

Turn messages into tasks

Delegate work to Synced straight from a conversation, then follow its progress and review the finished result in one place.

Connects to everything you use

Email, messages, cloud storage, calendar, and more, all synced together so work gets done without switching apps.

Synced prompts you

Instead of you prompting Synced, Synced comes to you with the decisions only you can make, then handles everything else.
